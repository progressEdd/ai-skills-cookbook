# Looking at examples and adapting prompting frameworks
> Don't invent prompts from scratch — study what works, steal the patterns, adapt them to you.

## Learning objectives
- [ ] Read a prompting framework and identify its core pattern (role, constraints, output format, loop)
- [ ] Compare at least two frameworks and articulate what each optimizes for
- [ ] Adapt a framework's pattern into your own reusable prompt or skill
